Overview

The Receipts agent is designed to process the family’s receipts systematically. It connects receipt photos with an organised record while keeping the underlying documents private.

Core workflow

  • CaptureReceipt images are collected through Apple Photos.
  • ProcessChatGPT supports structured extraction and handling.
  • OrganiseProcessed information is maintained in Google Drive.
  • ProtectOriginal receipts and private financial details are not published.
