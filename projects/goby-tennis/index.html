Overview

GOBY Tennis gives players one place to ask about matches, substitutes, rules and fellow players. The solution also supports AI-based match planning.

Core features

  • Online tennis chatPlayers can ask about matches, substitutes, rules and other players.
  • AI match planningAI supports the planning of matches and player line-ups.
  • Connected knowledgeChatGPT works with structured information held in Google Drive.
  • Python workflowPython supports the project’s processing and automation.
