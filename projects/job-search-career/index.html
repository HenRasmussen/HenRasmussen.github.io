Overview

Job Agent creates a structured flow for discovering and processing opportunities across 90–100 career sites. The project has been live since November 2025 and continues to evolve.

Core features

  • Broad searchThe agent searches 90–100 career sites for opportunities.
  • Posting processingJob postings are collected and processed into a consistent structure.
  • Opportunity overviewResults are organised in Google Drive for review and follow-up.
  • Connected workflowChatGPT, Gmail and Python support the end-to-end process.
