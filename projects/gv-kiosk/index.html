Overview

GV Kiosk supports the practical work involved in running a kiosk for Gentofte Volley’s league matches, combining operational records with AI-assisted calculations and planning.

Core features

  • Purchase registrationPurchases are recorded as part of the shared operational overview.
  • Inventory countsStock counts provide the basis for sales calculations and future needs.
  • Purchase planningThe solution calculates what should be bought for upcoming activity.
  • Offer-led purchasingPromotional flyers are used to support purchasing decisions.
